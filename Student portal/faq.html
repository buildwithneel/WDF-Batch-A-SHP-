Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is an online portal that helps students manage academic and campus-related activities in one place.


Who can use StudentHub?

Only registered students, faculty members, and administrators of the institution can access StudentHub.


How do I create an account?

Click on the Register page, fill in your details, and submit the registration form.

Account Related

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the "Forgot Password" option on the Login page to reset your password.


Can I update my profile information?

Yes. After logging in, visit the Profile page to update your personal information.


How can I change my password?

Go to your Profile page after logging in and select the Change Password option.

Events

How do I register for an event?

Open the Events page, select the desired event, and click the Register button.


Will I receive confirmation after registration?

Yes. A confirmation message will be displayed after successful registration.

Support

How can I contact StudentHub support?

Visit the Contact page and submit your query using the contact form.


Where can I provide feedback?

You can visit the Feedback page and share your suggestions or report any issues.
